1. Create a session

  1. Tap New session and give it a name, e.g. "Goa Trip" or "Flat Rent".
  2. Add the names of everyone involved (minimum 2 people).

2. Add expenses one by one

  1. Open your session and tap Add expense.
  2. Enter the amount, who paid, and who it should be split between.
  3. Choose Equal split or set custom amounts for each person.
  4. Each expense is added one at a time — you can keep adding more as bills come in.

3. Edit or delete an expense

  • Tap the pencil icon on any expense to change its details.
  • Tap the bin icon to remove it permanently.

4. Check balances and settle up

  • Balances tab shows who is owed money and who owes money.
  • Settle up tab shows the simplest set of payments to clear all debts.
  • Once someone pays you back, tap Money received to mark that payment as done.

5. Save your data

  • Go to Settings and tap Download to save a backup file (.json).
  • Use Upload to restore from a backup file.
