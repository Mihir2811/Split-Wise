1. Create a session

  1. Tap New session on the Home tab and give it a name — e.g. "Goa Trip" or "Flat Rent June".
  2. Add the names of everyone sharing expenses (minimum 2 people). You can always come back to the same session as new bills arrive.

2. Navigate using the bottom bar

  • Home — returns you to the sessions list and Quick Split at any time.
  • Sessions — jumps straight to your saved sessions list.
  • Quick Split — scrolls to the one-off split calculator below.
  • Settings — toggle dark mode, back up your data, or restore from a backup file.
  • About — info about the app, links to GitHub and LinkedIn.

3. Add expenses

  1. Open a session and tap Add expense.
  2. Enter the amount, a short description (e.g. "Dinner"), who paid, and who to split it between.
  3. Choose Equal split (default) or enter custom amounts per person.
  4. Tap Add expense to save. Add as many as you like — the app keeps a running total.

4. Edit or delete an expense

  • Tap the pencil icon on any expense card to update its details.
  • Tap the bin icon to permanently remove an expense.

5. Check balances and settle up

  • Switch to the Balances tab inside a session to see each person's net position — who is owed money and who still owes.
  • Switch to the Settle up tab to see the fewest payments needed to clear every debt. Each payment card shows exactly which shared expenses caused it.
  • Once someone transfers money, tap Money received on that card. Tap Undo if you marked it by mistake.

6. Share a session as PDF or Excel

  • Inside any session, tap Share as PDF / Excel (next to the Delete button).
  • Choose PDF for a print-ready report — open the downloaded .html file and use your browser's Print → Save as PDF option, then share via WhatsApp, email, or any other app.
  • Choose Excel / CSV for a structured spreadsheet with three sections: All Expenses, Balances, and Settle Up. Opens directly in Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers.

7. Back up and restore your data

  • Go to Settings (bottom bar) and tap Download to save a .json backup of all your sessions.
  • Tap Upload in Settings to restore from that backup file — useful when switching devices.
  • Inside a session, tap Download as text to save a plain-text summary of all expenses, balances, and settlement details.

8. Quick split

  • Use the Quick Split section (tap the Quick Split icon in the bottom bar, or scroll down on Home) for a fast one-off calculation you don't need to save.
  • Enter the total amount, the number of people, optional names, and what the expense was for — then tap Calculate split.
